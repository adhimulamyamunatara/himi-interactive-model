HIMI – Human Inspired Multimodal Interaction Model
Project Title
HIMI – Human Inspired Multimodal Interaction Model
A Human-Centered AI Learning Companion for Engineering Students

Tagline
Beyond Chatbots — Building an AI Companion that Sees, Listens, Understands, Remembers, and Teaches Like a Human Mentor.

Project Vision
Artificial Intelligence has significantly improved access to information through chatbots and voice assistants. However, today's AI systems are primarily reactive—they wait for users to ask questions and then generate responses.
Human teachers, mentors, and friends communicate very differently. They observe, listen, remember previous interactions, recognize confusion, adapt explanations, and guide learners naturally.
HIMI aims to bridge this gap by creating a Human-Inspired Multimodal Interaction Model that combines vision, voice, reasoning, memory, and conversational intelligence into a unified AI learning companion.
Instead of behaving like another chatbot, HIMI is designed to behave like a personal mentor.

Problem Statement
Current AI assistants excel at answering questions but provide limited personalized interaction for students.
Existing systems generally:
Require users to initiate every interaction.
Depend mainly on text or voice prompts.
Do not maintain long-term educational context.
Cannot continuously adapt to an individual learner.
Offer limited visual or conversational engagement.
As a result, engineering students often receive answers but miss the personalized guidance, contextual teaching, and interactive learning experience that human mentors naturally provide.
There is a need for a human-inspired AI companion capable of observing, understanding, remembering, reasoning, and teaching in a natural manner.

Solution Statement
HIMI introduces a Human-Inspired Multimodal Interaction Model.
The system combines:
Computer Vision
Speech Recognition
Large Language Models
AI Reasoning
Long-Term Memory
Animated Virtual Avatar
to create an intelligent learning companion that provides a more natural educational experience.
The objective is not to replace teachers but to provide every engineering student with a personal AI mentor available anytime.

Current Workflow
Student:
"Explain Operating Systems."

HIMI Workflow

HIMI Avatar:
"Good Morning, Bhargav.
Yesterday we completed CPU Scheduling.
Would you like to continue with Process Synchronization?"
Student:
"Yes."
During the explanation:
HIMI remembers previous lessons.
Adjusts explanations to the learner's pace.
Uses diagrams where necessary.
Tracks completed topics.
Stores learning history.
The interaction resembles learning from a mentor instead of chatting with software.
